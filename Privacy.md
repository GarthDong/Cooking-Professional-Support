# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 25 September 2026**

Cooking Professional is a professional kitchen management application for managing recipes, ingredients, inventory, procurement information, and production planning.

## Data Storage

Cooking Professional is designed as a local application. Information entered into the app, including recipes, ingredient information, inventory records, supplier and procurement information, and production plans, is stored locally on the user's device.

The current version of Cooking Professional does not require a user account and does not transmit this operational data to the developer.

## Data Collection

Cooking Professional does not collect personal information through the app.

The current version does not use third-party advertising, behavioural tracking, or third-party analytics services.

## Third-Party Sharing

Cooking Professional does not sell user data or share information entered into the app with advertisers or other third parties.

## Data Removal

Because application data is stored locally, users can remove data using the application's available editing and deletion functions. Removing the application from the device may also remove locally stored application data.

## Future Features

Future versions of Cooking Professional may introduce optional online or cloud-based functionality. If such functionality changes how user information is collected, transmitted, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
