# Cooking Professional

**A practical kitchen management tool built for professional cooking workflows.**

Cooking Professional is an iPhone and iPad application designed to help chefs and kitchen staff organise recipes, ingredients, inventory, procurement information, and production planning in one place.

The application is currently under active development and industry testing.

## Features

Cooking Professional currently includes:

- Structured recipe creation and management
- Ingredient library with common culinary ingredients and attributes
- Recipe scaling and ingredient quantity calculations
- Ingredient-specific unit conversions
- Allergen information and assessment
- Inventory tracking and inventory history
- Supplier and procurement information
- Kitchen production planning
- Recipe categories and organisation
- Local on-device data storage

## Designed for the Kitchen

Cooking Professional is designed around practical professional-kitchen workflows rather than general consumer recipe browsing.

The current release focuses on fast access to operational information, flexible recipe management, ingredient calculations, and local reliability.

## Privacy

The current version of Cooking Professional operates locally and does not require a user account.

Recipe, inventory, supplier, procurement, and production-planning information is stored on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

For more information, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
